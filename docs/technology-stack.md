# Home technology section

`TechnologyStack` appears between the portfolio and the about introduction, with a `/#stack` navigation anchor. The white section groups the complete stack supplied directly by the owner in `apps/web/lib/professional-profile.ts`; AWS, Microsoft Azure and Google Cloud Platform are included at the owner's request. No proficiency percentages or certifications are inferred.
